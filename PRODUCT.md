# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS deployed from `public/` on Netlify, with a small Node server retained for local preview and optional Replit use.

## Users

Primary: Startups and mid-size operators in Canada and the United States that use QuickBooks Online Advanced, are moving to it from Plus, or are about to buy it and need the file implemented so Advanced features actually get used.

Secondary: Umair as inbound qualifier; Taya as escalation for larger or complex opportunities; Ammar as founder/operator trust surface; internal NeuralSync team reviewing this draft before any public launch.

## Product Purpose

This page is a team-review landing page for NeuralSync remote accounting. It must build enough trust to start a contact-form request for a free 30-minute consultation, then collect a scoped lead. It is not a public launch, ad destination, or pricing commitment.

Success for this release: the team can see the buyer path, test the interactive close-health widget, amend copy/proof, and share a Netlify URL for feedback.

## Positioning

NeuralSync implements QuickBooks Online Advanced for startups and growing businesses so classes, custom reports, workflows, permissions, and month-end close actually get used. Intuit sells the software. NeuralSync makes the file operable.

Entry offers: Advanced file setup, turning on unused Advanced features, bookkeeping and close on that file, custom reports, workflows and permissions, and dedicated remote staff. FP&A stays later.

## Operating Context

- Visual authority: NeuralSync official logo plus the dark navy / ultraviolet / cyan / coral BPO visual kit.
- Conversion: contact form for a free 30-minute consultation. Primary CTAs scroll to `#inquiry`.
- Inbound: Umair filters. Complex prospects may later route to Taya. No CRM mutation and no external send from this draft.
- Draft-only controls stay hidden outside localhost unless the team adds `?review=1`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One primary CTA: book a free 30-minute consultation.
- Form qualifies with three fields: name, mandatory work email, and one need dropdown.
- Pricing dollar claims are hidden.
- Testimonials stay out of the rendered page until approved customer proof exists. The current trust section explains the fit, scope, control, and evidence boundaries instead.
- Tax language is coordination within a confirmed jurisdiction, not a cross-border filing promise.
- theBPO name, people, offices, and 2023 deck statistics are not used as public proof.
- Generated team and artwork images are labeled illustrative.

## Brand Commitments

- Market-facing brand for this page: NeuralSync.
- Official particle-cluster mark and wordmark from the NeuralSync site.
- Atmosphere from the BPO visual starter: midnight navy, violet, cyan, coral.
- Domain reference: neuralsync.ca, to verify before any public use.
- Do not copy The Value Core wording, layout, clients, or claims.
- Do not copy Intuit or QuickBooks landing-page wording, promotions, prices, testimonials, or screenshots. Structural inspiration only. NeuralSync does not resell Intuit licenses.

## Evidence on Hand

- NeuralSync logo SVGs.
- Original generated artwork: finance orbit, remote-team concept, ledger-to-clarity. Illustrative only.
- Source-backed service menu from the visual kit and launch control. Not client outcomes.
- No approved testimonials, named cases, or measured results are on hand. None are rendered or shipped.

## Product Principles

1. Outcome before mechanics.
2. Proof beside claims, and empty space when proof is missing.
3. One conversation CTA, repeated, never a maze of offers.
4. Entry accounting first; FP&A stays an expansion.
5. Team-review honesty over launch theater.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-usable form and navigation, visible focus, text contrast on the dark field, form errors that name the problem, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
