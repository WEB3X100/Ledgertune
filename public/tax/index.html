Questions owners ask.

What does ‘tax-ready’ actually mean?

Books fully reconciled, categories reviewed with tax in mind, deductible expenses identified and documented, and a clean package the accountant opens and starts from, with no back-and-forth or cleanup first.

Do you file my taxes?

No. We prepare everything the accountant needs to file accurately. The return is prepared and filed by the accountant who files. Ledger Tune does not take the return as CPA of record.

When is the return due?

For a December 31 year-end in Canada: the T2 return is due six months after year-end, and the balance owing is due two months after year-end, three months for a corporation eligible for the small business deduction. T4 and T5 slips are due February 28. Confirm the dates with the filing accountant.

My books are behind. Can you still get me tax-ready?

Yes. Catch-up comes first, scoped after a look at the file, then the tax-ready package is built. Starting in November or early December leaves room to finish before filing season.
