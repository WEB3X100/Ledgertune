Why month-end still steals the evening

Industry research brief. Not a Ledger Tune client result. Sources linked below.

Numeric looked at when 1,000 accountants using its software marked month-end closing tasks complete in 2023. 34% of those tasks were finished after 7 p.m. Only 18% were done by 10 a.m. The Journal of Accountancy reported the finding in January 2024.

That is not “most people always stay late.” It is a hard count of close work landing after dinner. Combined with FloQast’s UK finding that 87% of accountants reported increased stress during month-end close (The Accountant, 2023), the pattern is the same: the file is not carrying the month, so people are.

Ledger Tune’s response is operational, not a savings claim: recurring bills and invoices as workflows, categorization reviewed by a person, and a named reviewer on the close — so the books live in QuickBooks Online Advanced instead of a Sunday sitting.

Sources:

  • Journal of Accountancy — Accountants after dark (Numeric, 2024)
  • The Accountant — FloQast month-end stress finding (2023)
