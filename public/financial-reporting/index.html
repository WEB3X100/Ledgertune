Monthly accounting
and financial reporting.

A report is only useful when it comes from a reconciled file. Ledger Tune prepares the monthly statements you asked for, and labels anything that is still an estimate.

Book your free call All services

Where this usually starts.

  • The profit and loss changes every time someone recodes last month.
  • Cash in the bank and profit are being treated as the same number.
  • There is no comparison to the prior month.
  • A board pack is a spreadsheet rebuilt by hand.

Work included.

  • Profit and loss for the month and year to date
  • Balance sheet at the close date
  • Cash-flow statement or a cash schedule, when the file supports it
  • Prior-period comparison
  • Budget versus actual if you have a budget we can load
  • A short written note on the lines that moved

How the QuickBooks work actually runs.

Reports are issued after the reconciliations in scope are done. If an account is still open, the report says so. We do not present a clean pack on top of an unfinished bank.

What you receive.

  • The statement pack for the period
  • The QuickBooks reports those numbers came from
  • Notes on open items that affect the figures

Who it is for.

Owners who need a monthly view for themselves, a partner, or a lender. Best paired with monthly bookkeeping. A one-off report on an unreconciled file will be labeled as draft.

Three steps.

Step 1Name the readers and the comparisons they need.
Step 2Close the accounts that the pack depends on.
Step 3Send the pack with the open items beside it.

Credentials, ProAdvisor badges, and staff portraits are not shown here. They are added only when the person and the credential are verified.

Questions on this service.

Can the pack include forecasts?

A forecast is not part of standard reporting. If we add one later, it will be marked as a projection, with the assumptions listed.

Will lenders accept these statements?

They are management statements from QuickBooks. A review or audit engagement, if a lender requires one, is a separate professional service.
