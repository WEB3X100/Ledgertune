Monthly bookkeeping for
small businesses.

Monthly bookkeeping is the recurring version of a clean file. Transactions get coded, banks and cards get reconciled, and the month closes on a checklist.

Book your free call All services

Where this usually starts.

  • Coding happens in a rush the night before a meeting.
  • The credit card has not been reconciled this year.
  • Invoices and deposits do not meet.
  • You are the bookkeeper, and you are also running the company.

Work included.

  • Transaction categorization from statements and documents
  • Bank and credit-card reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable tracking
  • Invoice and payment reconciliation
  • Monthly close preparation
  • Document filing in the agreed place
  • Sales-tax coding support, not a tax filing

How the QuickBooks work actually runs.

You send documents on a rhythm, or we work from the feed plus what you upload. Each month has a cutoff. After cutoff, we reconcile, clear what we can, and send the open items with the reports in scope.

What you receive.

  • Reconciled accounts for the month
  • Coded transactions
  • Open items that need you
  • The reports named in the engagement

Who it is for.

Owner-managed companies, agencies, professional firms, and other growing businesses that have outgrown a spare-evening close. A good fit after cleanup, or from day one if the file is already trustworthy.

Three steps.

Step 1Set the accounts, the cutoff, and the documents we need.
Step 2Run the month against that checklist.
Step 3Review open items with you and roll to the next close.

Questions on this service.

Do I still need to send receipts?

Yes, when a transaction cannot be coded from the statement alone. The list of what we need is part of the scope.

Is payroll included?

Payroll accounting entries can be recorded from reports you provide. Running payroll and payroll filings are not part of bookkeeping unless a separate scope says so.
