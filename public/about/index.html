About Ledger Tune.

Ledger Tune keeps QuickBooks Online files accurate for Canadian small businesses. The practice sets up the file, cleans up a backlog, reconciles the accounts, and stays on for the monthly close when you want that.

The work is concentrated where the service can actually be delivered, including Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville, and the wider Greater Toronto Area, and for other Canadian businesses when the file can be handled remotely.

What we will not put on this page yet.

No staff names, no headshots, no years-in-business claim, no CPA designation, and no QuickBooks ProAdvisor badge until each of those facts is confirmed and the person has agreed to be published. A team grid of invented accountants would be worse than a plain explanation.

What you can count on in the work.

  • QuickBooks Online remains your system of record.
  • Reconciliations tie to statements.
  • Scope is written before the work starts.
  • Your accountant can stay your accountant.
  • Tax filing is not slipped in under the word bookkeeping.
