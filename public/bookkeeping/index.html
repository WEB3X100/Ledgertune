Bookkeeping that lands the month.

What is messy

Banks and cards do not match. Bills and open invoices sit while the month ends in a spreadsheet.

What we do

We post the month, reconcile banks and cards to statements, and review open invoices and bills. We also set up QuickBooks, a clear chart of accounts, and reporting views that keep the close easy to follow.

What you still own

You keep access to the file. Payroll filings and a tax return are separate work, named in the engagement.
