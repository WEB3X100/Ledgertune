Questions owners ask.

What is included in monthly bookkeeping, and what is not?

Coding, bank and card reconciliation, open invoices and bills, payroll journals, the month-end close, and the reports the month needs. Tax filing and CFO-level advice are separate services.

Do I still need an accountant if I have a bookkeeper?

Yes. Different roles. The bookkeeper keeps the books and prepares tax-ready schedules. The accountant advises and files the return. We work with the accountant you already have.

Do I keep ownership of my books?

Yes. The books live in your own QuickBooks Online file, and you keep access. If you leave, the file stays yours.

My books are months behind. Can you still take me on?

Yes. It starts with a look at the file. Cleanup or catch-up is scoped first, then the monthly close begins once the file is current.
