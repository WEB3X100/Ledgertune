Privacy on this preview.

The consultation form asks for your name, work email, company, an optional phone number, the service you need, the software you use, how far behind the books are, and a short description.

On this local preview, those details are written to a file on the computer running the site. They are not emailed, and they are not sent to an advertising network. A production inbox will be named here before the form is described as delivered.

Do not send social insurance numbers, banking passwords, or full card numbers through the form. QuickBooks access is arranged separately, after an engagement exists.

Questions about a submission can be raised on the same call. There is no published street address or public inbox on this preview.
