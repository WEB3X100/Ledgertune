Reconciliation for banks
and credit cards.

Reconciliation is the proof that QuickBooks agrees to the bank and the card. Ledger Tune does that work to the statement, month by month, for catch-up or as part of the monthly close.

Book your free call All services

Where this usually starts.

  • The reconciliation screen has been stuck for a year.
  • Transfers between accounts are counted twice.
  • The credit card payment is an expense and also a payment.
  • The feed brought in pending charges that then posted again.

Work included.

  • Statement-to-ledger match for each account in scope
  • Outstanding cheque and deposit tracking
  • Duplicate feed items
  • Credit-card payments mapped to the card account, not to an expense
  • A remaining-difference list when the statement cannot be fully tied

How the QuickBooks work actually runs.

We work from the PDF or the online statement, not from the feed alone. Each month is finished before the next one starts. A difference that lacks a document is reported, not plugged.

What you receive.

  • Reconciliations through the agreed statement date
  • A list of unmatched items
  • The QuickBooks reconciliation reports for your file

Who it is for.

Any QuickBooks Online client whose bank or card does not tie. This can be a focused project or the backbone of monthly bookkeeping.

Three steps.

Step 1List the accounts and the last statement that tied, if one ever did.
Step 2Reconcile forward, month by month.
Step 3Leave you with matched accounts and the items still open.

Questions on this service.

Is a connected bank feed the same as a reconciliation?

No. A feed downloads activity. A reconciliation agrees the book to a statement balance on a date.

Can you reconcile a year at once?

We still do it month by month. Skipping to the newest statement hides which month broke.
