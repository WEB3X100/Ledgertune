QuickBooks cleanup
and catch-up bookkeeping.

If the balance does not match the bank, or the books stopped months ago, cleanup is the engagement. It is also the usual door into monthly bookkeeping.

Book your free call All services

Where this usually starts.

  • The bank reconciliation has been open for months.
  • The same expense appears twice.
  • Undeposited funds never clears.
  • Nobody can explain retained earnings.
  • Tax season is the first time anyone opens the file.

Work included.

  • Historical transaction review through an agreed date
  • Bank and credit-card reconciliation
  • Duplicate and uncategorized transaction cleanup
  • Accounts receivable and payable review
  • Balance sheet cleanup for items the documents support
  • A handoff list for your accountant

How the QuickBooks work actually runs.

We lock a catch-up end date. Each bank and card is reconciled to a statement, not to a hunch. Items without paperwork stay open and visible. We do not journal the difference away to force a zero.

What you receive.

  • Reconciled accounts through the agreed date
  • An open-items list
  • A file your accountant can start from
  • A recommendation on whether monthly bookkeeping should follow

Who it is for.

Businesses with a backlog, a previous bookkeeper's unfinished file, or statements that have never been tied out. Bring the statements and the access. We cannot reconstruct a month that has no records.

Three steps.

Step 1See how far behind the file is and which accounts matter.
Step 2Reconcile and clean through the date in the scope.
Step 3Hand you the open items and, if you want it, a monthly plan.

Questions on this service.

Can every messy file be repaired?

Not if the source documents are gone. We repair what the statements, invoices, and bills support, and we label the rest.

Does cleanup include monthly service?

No. Cleanup ends at the agreed date. Monthly bookkeeping is a separate engagement that often starts the next day.
