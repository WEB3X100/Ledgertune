# Design

NeuralSync remote accounting landing. Dark finance-orbit world inherited from the BPO visual kit and the official NeuralSync particle-cluster mark.

## Surface

Persuade. Team-review draft. One page. Primary action is a contact form for a free 30-minute consultation.

## World

Midnight navy field (`#070814`). Coral consultation pills (`#ff6f61`). Cyan operating marks (`#6ee7ff`). Violet from the NeuralSync logo (`#6025e1`). Instrument Serif for display, Sora for interface. Generated orbit, team, and ledger artwork are atmosphere, labeled illustrative.

## First viewport

Split hero. Outcome headline and two CTAs on the left. Finance-orbit artwork at viewport scale on the right. A compact close-health widget occupies the open side of the dashboard image, switches between month-end and cash views, and labels all values as demo data. On mobile it moves beneath the artwork. Review controls appear only on localhost or with `?review=1`.

## Signature interaction

Service “Ask about this” links prefill the need and jump to the consultation form. Submit emails a request for a free 30-minute consultation. Mobile keeps a sticky consultation bar until the form is in view.

## Conversion

One primary CTA, repeated in the hero, after services, after process, in the nav, and on the mobile dock. Trust marks sit under the hero actions. A two-row icon marquee under the hero shows the stack we implement (reports, classes, workflows, consultation), not partner logos. Field errors sit next to the problem. The fit-check section carries trust through explicit operating boundaries until approved proof exists.

## Proof

A two-column assurance grid after the convert band explains file fit, scope, access control, and accepted evidence. No customer identity or outcome appears until it is approved.

## Constraints

No public pricing numbers. Tax language is coordination, not filing. `noindex`. Draft-only until Umair approves publication.
